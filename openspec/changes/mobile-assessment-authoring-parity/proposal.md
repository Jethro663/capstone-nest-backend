## Why

Mobile assessment saving rejects valid updates, can partially overwrite settings, and hides useful validation errors. AI drafts omit academic and delivery settings, while teacher navigation does not explain academic restrictions.

## What Changes

- Atomic, revision-aware, idempotent editor saving across web and mobile, with incomplete drafts and strict publication validation.
- Full grouped AI assessment settings persisted through job creation, review, retry and backend-owned apply.
- Forms-style mobile Questions / Settings / Preview editing, rich text, recovery and actionable academic restrictions.
- Safe destination-period mapping and reviewable historical repair reporting.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `assessment-authoring-parity`: Safe cross-platform manual and AI assessment authoring.

### Modified Capabilities
None. Existing academic restrictions remain authoritative.

## Impact

Nest assessments and AI orchestration, Drizzle migrations, Python AI contracts, web assessment and AI editors, mobile assessment/navigation/recovery and rich-text dependencies. No production data repair or deployment.
